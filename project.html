Market Research: Pickleball Facility & Event Lead Generation

Role: Data Entry & Lead Generation Specialist | Timeline: January 2025 – August 2026

Project Overview: Designed and compiled a comprehensive B2B lead generation database tracking the rapidly expanding Pickleball market across North America. The project involved sourcing and verifying data for dedicated facilities and upcoming tournament events to facilitate targeted outreach.

Dataset Metrics (Sanitized)

  • Verified Club Facilities: 188 distinct locations identified.
  • Upcoming & Past Tournaments: 114 regional events tracked.
  • Top Targeted Markets: Florida, California, and Texas.
  • Data Cleaned: 100% of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) removed for public portfolio display to ensure compliance and privacy.
